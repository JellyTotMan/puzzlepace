Privacy policy

Puzzle Pace uploader · last updated 14 October 2026

What this app is

The Puzzle Pace uploader is a personal tool run by the owner of the Puzzle Pace YouTube channel on their own computer. It is not offered to the public and no one else signs in to it.

What it accesses

With the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth), it uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set thumbnails and captions, and add videos to playlists on the owner's own channel. It does not read, collect or store data about any other person.

Storage and sharing

The access token is stored only on the owner's computer. No data is sold, shared or sent to any third party other than Google/YouTube as needed to publish the videos.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
